# IBD transport crate

Geometry authored for this project. Native source and reproducible build: `docs/world/phase-2-playground/ibd-crate-craft-20261101/` and `tools/world/ibd-crate-craft/`.

Timber maps derive from Poly Haven [Coated Pine](https://polyhaven.com/a/coated_pine), [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The project selects a continuous board region and authors lighter color and matte roughness; these adaptations are not claimed as a scan of untreated packaging timber. Original URLs, author attribution, source hashes and adaptation rules are recorded in `tools/world/ibd-crate-craft/textures/sources.json`. End grain is authored procedurally. No third-party geometry, logo, animation or runtime lights are included.

Runtime: 23,480 triangles, 8 shared materials / 11 primitives; independent Shell and Lid origins, metres, Y up. Short-range UV1 AO excludes rubber. GLB SHA-256 `a01de057e34cc1788684d0874b5da50682ab9fdfeae2f794a5140411b207723e`.
