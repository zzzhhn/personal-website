# Camp display pads and exit lamp posts

C02 uses six quarry-split slabs in two staggered runs over a compacted bed. The tops stay flat at the existing prop support level. Quiet 22 mm joints, eased chipped arrises and a lower side course describe the layup. The stone color varies gently over the face and the finer pores are in normal and roughness.

L01 keeps the timber shaft and its cyan lens. The stone socket supports a broad anchored shoe with four visible fasteners. A collar, mullions, lower and upper lens seats and lapped rain hood make up the light head. Its dark coated metal and warmer fastener material separate the hardware from the oiled timber.

## Source and runtime

[Native Blender source](camp-site-v1.blend) has named editable objects and packed source maps. Its roots are moved apart for editing. `build.py` builds in meters with Z up and writes the Y-up [runtime GLB](../../../public/assets/world/camp-site-craft/camp-site-v1.glb) with both roots restored to local origin. All maps are original deterministic procedural PNGs generated by `surfaces.py`. Tiling is intentional. Each slab samples a different offset, and timber UVs follow the length of the shaft.

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --threads 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/world/camp-site-craft/build.py
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --threads 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/world/camp-site-craft/review.py -- --night
```

Requires Blender 5.1.2 and the local `blender-material-craft` skill. Set `BLENDER_MATERIAL_CRAFT` to the skill root if it is elsewhere. [Manifest](manifest.json) records first complete hashes, source inputs and the shared map set.

The 971,548-byte runtime GLB has six material primitives, 2,040 unique triangles and six embedded 512 × 512 PNGs. The stone material and its maps are shared between C02 and the L01 foot. `WorldCampSiteCraft` instantiates the stone pad batch eight times and five post batches twice. The resulting 5,928 geometry triangles are a count before culling or extra passes. The adapter keeps the GLB's base color, vertex tint, normal and roughness channels, uses the existing cached outdoor PMREM and drives the lens from the original `0.18 + night.value * 1.25` intensity curve. Its material and instance buffers are scoped to the mount. Loader geometry, embedded texture objects and the shared reflection cache retain their existing owners. `?campSiteCraft=legacy` selects the local visual fallback for diagnostics.

## Evidence

- Matched daytime [baseline](review/01-baseline-day.png), [native candidate](review/02-native-day.png) and [fresh GLB import](review/04-reimport-day.png). The offset joints and eased perimeter are visible at the chosen distance. The pole reads as timber in a separate mounted shoe and its lens housing is clearer. Surface pores and fasteners are close-range details.
- [Clay construction view](review/03-native-clay.png) shows the slab beds, bearing faces, foot and cap without surface maps.
- Matched night [baseline](review/05-baseline-night.png) and [fresh GLB import](review/06-reimport-night.png). The central cyan lens remains distinct from the hood and collar. The nearby warm review light is part of the common offline rig.
- [Scenario checks](scenario-report.json) found no flipped or zero-area UV faces on the mapped surfaces and no material errors. Stone density is about 394 px/m. Timber deliberately uses more pixels across grain than along it, so the larger UV angle difference is expected. All GLB triangles passed finite/nondegenerate checks.
- [Fresh import readback](review/readback.json) matches the source material inventory, triangle counts and bounds within 0.01 mm. The review shows the original L01 GLB and a reconstruction of C02 pad index zero in a common Blender rig. In-world contact, ordinary player view and day/night performance are evaluated by the camp integration.
