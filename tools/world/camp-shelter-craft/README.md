# Camp C04 and C10 craft pack

This pack refines the two existing small tents and the west flag mast in the warm, faceted camp. It uses the old GLBs from `3c66e8da538e83a9bbc56aa65592251b87764cca` as exact geometry and datum evidence. See [the frozen camp images](../../../docs/world/phase-2-playground/camp-batch-20261107/evidence/) for the ordinary game views and [the visual calibration contract](../../../docs/world/phase-2-playground/visual-calibration-20261027/README.md) for verification boundaries.

| Assembly | Construction | Preserved datum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C04 rain fly | Sagging cut canvas between the gables; rolled eaves, overlap seams, ridge cord and a turned weather apron; a small pull at each guy loop. Coarse cloth shape carries the reading at player distance. | Original A-frame, 3.4 × 4.4 m cell, 2.5 m finial and +X entrance. |
| C04 doorway | The original six-sided gable cut, two fluted curtains cinched with flax ties, two timber jambs with diagonal load paths and a grounded floor. | Original door shoulder and crown clearances; original hollow shell retained as fitted physics. |
| C10 mast | Tapered axial timber, recessed foot joinery with stays, dark iron straps and hoist bands, a spun brass finial. | Same base, pole axis, four hoist pivots, and mast height. |
| C10 flags | Thin closed cloth with lapped hems, light sag and a more readable ripple toward the loose fly. Four colors share one atlas. The hoist edge stays fixed to its pivot in each authored mesh. | Original pennant/swallow-tail sequence, 0.95 m length, 0.36 m hoist, hoist transforms, and the existing rust/cream/amber/olive palette. |

`camp-shelter-editable.blend` has separate named editable assemblies, derived runtime batches and an offline rig. Native Z is up; the export rotates to glTF Y-up. The bundle embeds the PNG maps, with the original maps also available in `public/assets/world/camp-shelter-craft/maps`. These are original procedural images released CC0, with no photographed material or outside assets. Canvas color and timber color use sRGB; roughness and normals use non-color. The 512 px flag atlas is a color map. Other maps are 256 px.

Reproduce from the repository root with the Blender Material Craft skill installed:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --threads 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/world/camp-shelter-craft/build.py
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --threads 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/world/camp-shelter-craft/review.py
```

The build regenerates the public runtime manifest and preserves the existing first-complete manifest. Scenario `uv.texel_density`, `materials.audit_material` and `export.glb_summary` record material and transport properties. Tiling UVs overlap by design. All named native meshes are closed and clear of degenerate faces by the included bmesh check. The Scenario warnings for uniform fractional metalness are retained in the audit; they reflect the stylized iron and brass response rather than claiming a measured pure metal surface. The native checks are structural, without the game's day/night lights, collisions or interaction paths.

The visual adapter exports `SmallTents({ placements })` and `FlagVisual({ placement })`. Keep both under the caller's original `camp-asset-*` parent with its fitted original physics and fallback. Two tents share five material batches and their matching depth patch. The cloth mesh arrives subdivided. A direct Three GLTFLoader check found two correctly classified moving curtains, with 380 nonzero curtain-mask vertices and preserved color/normal/roughness bindings. The C10 flag names, pivots and attachment metadata remain exact. Shared wind updates Y rotation while the world is running; existing interaction feedback owns X rotation independently. Both visuals borrow the renderer's existing reflection environment and loader image Sources. Only their own material/depth/geometry/sampler wrappers are disposed; mip filtering and anisotropy are bounded at four samples. No new lights or collision shapes are installed.

`first-complete-manifest.json` records the original generation, including the first C04 hash `cd792456…`. The source, GLB and affected offline views are preserved under [first-complete evidence](qa/first-complete/). That pass revealed a timber brace and top curtain shoulder pressing through the fly, and a thin gable edge slit. The final C04 adjusts the brace joint and narrows the upper curtain where it meets the roof, fits the gable seam to the curved fly, closes the wall corner seam and softens the repetitious fabric mottling. [The public runtime manifest](../../../public/assets/world/camp-shelter-craft/manifest.json) identifies the final files. The C10 GLB is byte-for-byte unchanged from its first complete export.

The final C04 has 8,440 triangles and five instanced batches for both tents. The old C04 draw count was also five. Its original 1,020 GLB triangles became 3,270 per tent after runtime tessellation (verified from the Draco-decoded baseline with the actual modifier). C10 has 4,296 triangles and eight meshes including the four articulated flags, up from 1,236 triangles across eight primitives. Across both tents and the mast, a full visible pass adds 13,400 model triangles with the same primitive count. These are topology counts; the coordinator measures runtime frame cost separately.

The [round-trip audit](qa/roundtrip.json) records the freshly imported asset bounds, all mesh UVs, and the exact door, wind and flag contract comparison. The offline views use identical camera, exposure, resolution, lighting and neutral ground within each asset's comparison: C04 [old](qa/C04-baseline.png), [native](qa/C04-source.png), [reimport](qa/C04-reimport.png), [clay](qa/C04-clay.png); C10 [old](qa/C10-baseline.png), [native](qa/C10-source.png), [reimport](qa/C10-reimport.png), [clay](qa/C10-clay.png). The offline C10 baseline deliberately uses the original GLB blue before the established game adapter's orange recolor.
