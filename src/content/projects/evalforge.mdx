## Product thesis

Automated metrics can assist video-model evaluation, but they do not replace human judgment on motion realism, visual coherence, instruction fidelity, or creative intent. The product challenge is therefore operational: how do you scale subjective review without losing data integrity, reviewer accountability, or the evidence behind a model decision?

EvalForge turns that challenge into a controlled workflow. It is a self-hosted, provider-neutral platform where evaluation setup, annotation, integrity review, model comparison, and reporting share one data model rather than living across spreadsheets and disconnected scripts.

## What I owned

- **Evaluation operations:** designed the flow from packages and assignments to workstation scoring, quality review, analytics, Arena comparison, and export.
- **Annotation experience:** built keyboard-driven multi-dimensional Likert scoring, structured failure tags, prompt snapshots, repeat sampling, session continuity, and configurable watch-progress requirements.
- **Data-integrity policy:** translated reviewer behavior into explicit, tunable rules while preserving suspicious records for human review and maintaining a recoverable audit trail.
- **Platform governance:** implemented multi-role Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), permission overrides, JWT httpOnly authentication, audit events, and request-context correlation.

## Trustworthy data, not just faster labeling

Scores of 1 or 2 require a structured failure reason before persistence. Watch ratio, duration-adjusted dwell time, hourly frequency, dominant-value patterns, and low variance provide complementary integrity signals. The live submission path and the recomputation path share the same classification logic, and administrator-confirmed invalid records are not overwritten by a later batch run.

This design separates three outcomes that are often conflated: a submission can be blocked for missing mandatory evidence, recorded as suspicious for review, or retained as valid. That distinction protects both throughput and explainability.

## Decision layer

EvalForge supports standard score aggregation as well as Arena evaluation between models or datasets. Ratings are updated in bounded, snapshot-consistent Glicko-2 batches, while reporting uses valid scores and preserved prompt or contestant context. Administrators can move from item-level evidence to dimension-level comparison, then export editable XLSX or DOCX outputs for further review.

The system also includes sample browsing, evaluation taxonomy management, storage integration, and bilingual administrative workflows. These are not isolated features; together they form an operating layer for running and auditing evaluation programs.

## Current scope and public-product boundary

The public repository deliberately excludes private generation workers, provider-specific orchestration, internal deployment history, credentials, and confidential scoring taxonomies. EvalForge remains an actively maintained functional prototype: its architecture is production-oriented, but this portfolio does not claim external adoption, live annotation volume, or independently audited security.

## 产品命题

自动化指标可以辅助视频模型评测，却无法替代人类对运动真实性、画面一致性、指令遵循与创作意图的判断。真正的产品难题因此不是“能否打分”，而是如何在扩大主观评审规模时，仍然保留数据质量、评审责任与模型决策背后的证据。

EvalForge 将这一难题转化为受控工作流。它是一套自托管、服务商中立的平台，让评测配置、人工标注、完整性复核、模型对比与报告共享同一套数据模型，不再散落于表格和彼此割裂的脚本之中。

## 我的核心职责

- **评测运营体系：** 设计从评测包、任务分配到工作台评分、质量复核、分析、Arena 对比与导出的完整流程。
- **标注员体验：** 构建键盘优先的多维 Likert 评分、结构化失败标签、Prompt Snapshot、重复抽样、会话连续性与可配置观看进度要求。
- **数据质量策略：** 将标注行为转化为明确、可调的规则，同时保留可疑记录供人工复核，并维持可恢复的审计链路。
- **平台治理：** 实现多角色 Role-Based Access Control（RBAC）、权限覆盖、JWT httpOnly 认证、审计事件与请求上下文关联。

## 追求可信数据，而不只是更快标注

当评分为 1 或 2 时，系统要求先提交结构化失败原因。观看比例、按视频时长调整的停留时间、小时级频率、单一分值主导与低方差模式共同构成完整性信号。在线提交与批量重算复用同一套分类逻辑，管理员已经确认的无效记录也不会被后续任务意外覆盖。

这一设计明确区分了三类结果：缺少必要证据时阻止提交，行为异常时保留并标记为可疑，满足规则时记录为有效。由此兼顾标注效率与结果可解释性。

## 决策支持层

EvalForge 同时支持常规分数聚合，以及模型或数据集之间的 Arena 评测。Rating 通过有迭代上限、快照一致的 Glicko-2 Batch 更新；报告仅使用有效评分，并保留 Prompt 与 Contestant 上下文。管理者可以从单条证据下钻至维度级对比，再导出可继续编辑的 XLSX 或 DOCX 报告。

样本浏览、评测维度管理、存储接入与双语后台并非孤立功能，而是共同组成一套用于运行和审计评测项目的运营基础设施。

## 当前边界与公开产品范围

公开仓库明确排除了私有生成 Worker、服务商专属编排、内部部署历史、凭证与机密评分体系。EvalForge 目前仍是持续维护中的功能原型，其架构具备面向生产的设计取向，但本页面不宣称外部采用规模、实时标注量或经过独立审计的安全性。
