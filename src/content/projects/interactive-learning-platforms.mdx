## Product thesis

Traditional courseware is optimized for presenting information. Students, however, need to decide what to study next, test whether they understand it, diagnose mistakes, and connect formulas to decisions. I built this portfolio of learning systems to close that gap: each course becomes an interactive environment for practice, feedback, visualization, and revision.

The four products share a lightweight browser-first philosophy, but they do not force every discipline into the same template. Finance uses calculators and decision models, differential equations uses dynamic visual laboratories, and business ethics uses frameworks, cases, and debate prompts.

## What I owned

- **Learning-product design:** translated syllabi, lecture material, and exam requirements into navigable learning paths and explicit student tasks.
- **Active-learning systems:** built quizzes, exam simulators, mastery dashboards, wrong-answer remediation, bookmarks, formula references, and targeted revision flows.
- **Interactive modelling:** implemented finance and valuation tools, portfolio diagnostics, ODE visualizations, knowledge maps, and scenario-based case work.
- **Distribution architecture:** packaged each platform as a self-contained HTML application that opens directly in a browser without a build step or installation.

## Four disciplines, four learning models

| Course | Learning problem | Product response |
|---|---|---|
| **FIN4210 · Corporate Finance** | Connect formulas, risk, and capital-allocation decisions | Guided learning packs, formula lab, knowledge map, quizzes, cases, mock exams, and progress tracking |
| **FIN3080 · Investment Analysis** | Compare valuation and portfolio frameworks under exam pressure | Ten-chapter quick pass, DDM/CAPM/DuPont/MPT labs, company screening, exam modes, variant drills, and wrong-answer vault |
| **MAT2002 · Differential Equations** | Build intuition for dynamic systems beyond symbolic steps | Unified ODE lab, slope fields, phase portraits, worked examples, exam navigation, mastery and weakness dashboards |
| **ACT4253 · Business Ethics** | Apply competing ethical frameworks to ambiguous situations | Chapter practice, knowledge map, short answers, case analysis, debate prompts, and framework reference |

## Engineering for low-friction learning

The products use semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript for direct delivery. MathJax, Plotly, Chart.js, or math.js are loaded where specialised rendering is required; progress, filters, bookmarks, and practice state are handled in the browser. This architecture avoids a deployment pipeline and keeps each course portable, while remaining honest that CDN-backed libraries are external dependencies rather than claiming a fully offline runtime.

The central engineering challenge was not file size. It was keeping large bodies of content navigable and stateful without a backend: definitions must precede interactions, cross-references must stay aligned, mathematical rendering must survive string escaping, and every learning mode must return the student to a meaningful next action.

## Current scope

FIN4210, FIN3080, and MAT2002 are the most developed systems. ACT4253 demonstrates the same product approach with a representative question set rather than a fully migrated bank. Bilingual support also varies by course. The portfolio is presented as completed coursework infrastructure, not as a validated commercial EdTech product or a claim of improved grades.

## 产品命题

传统课程材料擅长呈现信息，但学生真正需要解决的是：下一步学什么、是否已经理解、错误出在哪里，以及公式如何连接现实决策。我构建这组学习系统，正是为了补上这一缺口，让每门课程都成为可练习、有反馈、能可视化并支持复习的交互环境。

四套产品共享轻量、浏览器优先的理念，但不会强迫所有学科套用同一模板。金融课程使用计算器与决策模型，微分方程强调动态可视化实验，商业伦理则围绕分析框架、案例与辩论展开。

## 我的核心职责

- **学习产品设计：** 将教学大纲、课程材料与考试要求转化为可导航的学习路径和明确的学生任务。
- **主动学习系统：** 构建测验、考试模拟、掌握度 Dashboard、错题修复、收藏、公式速查与定向复习流程。
- **交互式建模：** 实现公司金融与估值工具、投资组合诊断、ODE 可视化、知识地图与情境化案例练习。
- **分发架构：** 将每套平台封装为自包含 HTML App，无需 Build Step 或安装即可直接在浏览器中打开。

## 四门课程，四种学习模型

| 课程 | 核心学习难点 | 产品回应 |
|---|---|---|

| **FIN3080 · 投资分析** | 在考试压力下比较估值与投资组合框架 | 十章 Quick Pass、DDM／CAPM／DuPont／MPT Lab、公司筛选、考试模式、变式训练与错题库 |
| **MAT2002 · 微分方程** | 超越符号步骤，建立对动态系统的直觉 | Unified ODE Lab、方向场、相图、逐步示例、考试导航、掌握度与弱项 Dashboard |
| **ACT4253 · 商业伦理** | 在模糊情境中运用并比较伦理框架 | 章节练习、知识地图、简答、案例分析、辩论题与框架速查 |

## 为低门槛学习而设计工程架构

产品以 Semantic HTML、CSS 与 Vanilla JavaScript 直接交付，并在需要专业渲染时加载 MathJax、Plotly、Chart.js 或 math.js；学习进度、筛选、收藏与练习状态均在浏览器内管理。该架构省去部署 Pipeline，使课程便于携带，同时也明确承认 CDN Library 属于外部依赖，而不是将其包装为完全离线运行。

真正的工程难题从来不是文件行数，而是如何在没有后端的前提下，让大规模课程内容仍然可导航、有状态。定义必须先于交互，跨章节引用必须保持一致，数学公式需要正确处理字符串转义，每种学习模式也必须把学生带回一个有意义的下一步动作。

## 当前边界

FIN4210、FIN3080 与 MAT2002 是目前完成度最高的系统；ACT4253 延续同一产品方法，但题库仍以代表性样本为主，并未完成全部迁移。各课程的双语覆盖也有所差异。这组作品体现的是已经完成的课程学习基础设施，而不是经过商业验证的 EdTech 产品，也不宣称已经直接提升成绩。
