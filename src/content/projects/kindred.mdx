## Product thesis

Most AI career tools optimize a single moment: answer a question, rewrite a résumé, or recommend a role. Accompany started from a different premise. A useful long-term companion should accumulate context, show where its conclusions came from, and help a student see how scattered experiences connect over time.

The product therefore treats conversation as an input to a living growth dossier rather than the final output. Chat, uploaded résumés, and user corrections converge into one evidence-backed profile graph that can support reflection and future action.

## What I owned

- **Product strategy:** defined the persistence-first experience for university students, connecting reflective conversation with a growth map instead of building another stateless chatbot.
- **Knowledge architecture:** designed the schema for skills, experiences, goals, people, relationships, confidence, source quotes, supersession, and manual edits.
- **AI pipeline:** built structured extraction, confidence filtering, deduplication, graph merging, community detection, and bounded insight generation.
- **Full-stack delivery:** implemented multi-session chat, resume ingestion, authentication, Bring Your Own Key (BYOK), bilingual UX, graph visualization, and shareable conversations.

## An evidence-backed growth graph

After a conversation is persisted, the extraction layer accepts only explicit evidence, applies a confidence floor, removes weak orphan signals, and validates structured output before it reaches the graph. New entities are compared with existing nodes rather than appended blindly. Mentions and confidence accumulate, while source messages remain traceable.

Weighted Louvain community detection groups related experiences and capabilities into themes. Insights are then bounded to structural observations such as bridge nodes, growing clusters, or missing connections. The system deliberately avoids claiming that the LLM has discovered a causal truth about the user.

Resume uploads in PDF, DOCX, or TXT enter the same pipeline through a dedicated résumé conversation. This allows documented history and newly articulated interests to coexist in one model, with streamed progress and background graph or memory reconciliation.

## Trust and privacy decisions

Accompany separates private session ownership from public sharing; shared views omit account identifiers. BYOK credentials are scoped to authenticated actions, stored with AES-256-GCM encryption, and shown only through masked metadata. Memory updates use soft deletion, graph stages are idempotent, and recomputation is transactionally anchored so user corrections can be preserved.

These choices matter because a career companion handles unusually personal context. The product should be able to explain what it knows, where it learned it, and how the user can correct it.

## Current scope

Accompany was developed through the Tencent AI-HR bootcamp and is now an archived functional prototype. The repository demonstrates a production-oriented foundation and a complete product thesis, but this portfolio does not claim validated user outcomes or ongoing maintenance. Historical internal identifiers remain in storage and migration code even though the user-facing brand is Accompany / 比邻.

## 产品命题

多数 AI 生涯工具只优化某个瞬间：回答一个问题、改写一份简历，或推荐一个岗位。比邻从另一个前提出发：真正长期有用的 Companion 应当持续积累上下文，说明结论从何而来，并帮助学生看见零散经历如何随时间形成联系。

因此，产品不把对话视为终点，而把它当作持续生长的个人档案入口。聊天、上传简历与用户修订最终汇入同一张有证据支持的成长图谱，为反思与后续行动提供上下文。

## 我的核心职责

- **产品策略：** 为大学生设计“持续积累优先”的体验，将反思式对话与成长地图连接起来，而不是再做一个无记忆 Chatbot。
- **知识架构：** 定义技能、经历、目标、人物、关系、置信度、原文引用、版本替代与人工修订的数据模型。
- **AI Pipeline：** 构建结构化抽取、置信度过滤、实体去重、图谱合并、社区发现与有边界的洞察生成。
- **全栈交付：** 实现多会话对话、简历导入、身份认证、Bring Your Own Key（BYOK）、双语界面、图谱可视化与会话分享。

## 有证据支持的成长图谱

对话持久化后，抽取层只接受明确出现的信息，执行置信度门槛与弱孤立信号过滤，并在写入图谱前校验结构化输出。新实体不会被盲目追加，而是先与既有节点比对；提及次数与置信度可以累积，来源消息则始终可追溯。

加权 Louvain Community Detection 将相关经历与能力组织为主题群落。系统随后仅基于桥接节点、成长中的 Cluster 或缺失连接等图结构形成观察，明确避免把 LLM 的推测包装为对用户的因果判断。

PDF、DOCX 与 TXT 简历会通过独立的“我的简历”会话进入同一套 Pipeline，使正式履历与逐步表达出的兴趣共同存在于一张图中，并通过流式进度和后台图谱／记忆校准完成处理。

## 信任与隐私设计

比邻将私有会话所有权与公开分享分离，分享视图不会暴露账号标识。BYOK 凭证限定在鉴权后的操作中，使用 AES-256-GCM 加密存储，界面仅显示掩码信息。记忆更新采用软删除，图谱阶段具备幂等性，重算由事务锚定，以保留用户修订。

这些选择格外重要，因为生涯伙伴天然涉及高度个人化的上下文。产品必须能够回答：它知道什么、从哪里得知，以及用户如何纠正。

## 当前边界

比邻源自腾讯 AI-HR 实战营，目前是停止维护的功能原型。仓库呈现了面向生产的基础能力与完整产品命题，但本页面不宣称已经验证用户结果或仍在持续维护。用户侧品牌已经统一为 Accompany／比邻，历史内部标识则仍保留在存储与迁移代码中。
