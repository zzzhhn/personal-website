Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a single cohesive three-view architectural concept board for a playable browser 3D world, landscape composition. This is a design target, not a screenshot.
Primary request: DJC BEACH CLUB, a medium-to-large open-air coastal music club that grows naturally from an existing island coast onto a broad sandy crescent peninsula with low timber decks projecting slightly over turquoise water. The land extension must look organic and connected, never a square platform or a separate floating island. This is a refined stylized handcrafted game environment, visually compatible with a warm limestone and timber coastal island, detailed readable forms and tactile materials, not photoreal architecture and not a low-poly toy.
Layout: one dominant large oblique aerial view at warm late-afternoon sunset occupying about 70% of the board; two smaller right-side views showing the SAME club at player eye height at blue hour and a close view of the DJ booth. Keep the layout and architecture consistent in all three views.
Site: broad curved sandy approach from island road through sea grasses, low planted dunes and a modest DJC sign; a generous oval/oyster-shaped timber dance floor, slightly sunken by two broad shallow steps with a wide accessible ramp. The land-side DJ booth faces the sea and dance floor, under one sculptural cream canvas sail on substantial weathered timber masts. One large mirrored disco ball hangs over the dance-floor center on believable cables. Layered curved limestone sitting edges, terracotta cushions, a compact side bar with pergola, relaxed shaded lounge pockets and a broad lower viewing deck toward the ocean. Two clearly navigable broad landward exits. Railings only at drop edges. No ornamental maze.
Near detail: an actual two-deck DJ controller with black faceplate, two jog wheels, small restrained cyan/amber displays, faders, knobs, pads, cables and monitor speakers; timber planks with subtle variation, softly chipped pale stone, brushed dark metal, linen shade cloth. A few sea grapes, coastal shrubs, flowering grasses and restrained palms frame ocean views rather than blocking them.
Lighting: beautiful warm natural sunlight and soft shadows in main view; in blue hour view warm architectural light, muted amber and teal stage accents, a small number of soft beams and disco reflections scattered on the dance floor, not a neon laser festival. Light sources have plausible mounts. Sea remains visible at night.
Scale: medium-to-large destination, approximately 65 by 50 meters, human-readable and buildable. Include a handful of small stylized visitors with simple white spherical helmets and navy clothing as scale references, some dancing, no crowds.
Text: only "DJC" on the venue sign and small tasteful board title "DJC / BEACH CLUB". No diagram labels or UI.
Constraints: prioritize architectural beauty, waterfront setting, useful empty dance space and cohesive materials. No swimming pool, no giant nightclub building, no city skyscrapers, no futuristic cyberpunk, no screens floating in the air, no carnival rides, no extra gameplay equipment, no floor full of holograms. Limit the design to one clear dance floor, one DJ stage, lounge and sea terrace.
