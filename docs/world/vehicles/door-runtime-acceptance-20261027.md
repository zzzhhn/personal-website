# Vehicle doors: runtime acceptance

Three vehicles now have separate hinged doors, genuine side openings and modeled cabins. Coupe doors rotate outward and upward together; offroad and van doors use conventional front hinges. Near and far assets share hinge/seat coordinates through `door-contract.json`. Driving dimensions, wheels, collision hulls and original material records are preserved.

## Interaction

Approach a stationary car and press **E**. The character approaches the selected side, waits for the door to open, ducks and transfers through the aperture, settles onto the seat and closes the door. Press **E** after stopping to reverse the sequence. Driver remains visible inside. An exit requested during entry is queued. Recovery closes any active door.

The existing CMU-derived sit/stand source is adapted to each cabin, with bone-local leg and torso adjustments and posterior contact alignment. This is not newly acquired vehicle-specific mocap. Hand-to-handle contact IK and independently simulated door collision bodies are not included.

Before entering or exiting, a bounded 13-pose door sweep checks nearby solids, excluding the car's own closed driving hull and the character. Exit candidates also retain the ground/capsule checks. This is sampled clearance, not a continuous collision guarantee.
