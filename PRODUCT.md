## Users and purpose

Recruiters, collaborators and technically curious visitors use this bilingual portfolio to understand Haonan Zhong's work, inspect projects and get in touch.

## Established direction

Energetic, tactile and exploratory. Preserve the existing Liquid Glass surfaces, animated strands, project fan, Chinese Songti headings and English typography. The user values the site's motion and does not want a generic static redesign.

## Change boundaries

Content additions reuse the existing timeline and theme tokens. English and Chinese entries and their workflows must remain paired. Public experience claims come from current user-confirmed materials, not earlier application drafts or assumed responsibilities.

Experimental Astra and 3D studies remain separate from the production site until accepted. Bruno's original source reproduction is a study with attribution, not a claim of original authorship.

## Accessibility and verification

Keep text selectable, workflow controls keyboard accessible, and content readable in both themes and on mobile. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Verify the changed experience in the built page and in the deployed assets; distinguish browser checks from real-device acceptance.
